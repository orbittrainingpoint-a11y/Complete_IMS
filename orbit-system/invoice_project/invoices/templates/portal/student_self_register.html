Terms & Conditions — Orbit Training Centre

Please read the following terms carefully before enrolling at Orbit Training Centre.

1. Enrollment & Registration

Enrollment is confirmed only upon receipt of the registration form and payment of the required fees. Orbit Training Centre reserves the right to accept or reject any registration at its sole discretion. Students are required to provide accurate personal information at the time of registration.

2. Payment Terms

All course fees must be paid in full prior to or on the first day of training, unless a term payment plan has been agreed in writing. Fees are quoted in UAE Dirhams (AED) and are subject to 5% VAT as per UAE Federal Tax Authority regulations.

3. Cancellation & Refund Policy

Cancellations made 7 or more days before the course start date are eligible for a full refund or transfer to another course. Cancellations made within 7 days of the start date are non-refundable. No-shows on the day of training forfeit the full course fee.

4. Attendance & Conduct

A minimum attendance of 80% is required to receive a certificate of completion. Students are expected to conduct themselves professionally and respectfully toward trainers and fellow participants.

5. Course Modifications

Orbit Training Centre reserves the right to modify course content, schedule, trainer, or delivery mode with reasonable prior notice. In the event of course cancellation by Orbit Training Centre, a full refund or transfer will be offered.

6. Certification

Certificates are issued only to students who meet the minimum attendance requirement and successfully complete any required assessments.

7. Confidentiality & Data Privacy

Personal information collected during registration is used solely for administrative and training purposes. Orbit Training Centre will not share your information with third parties without your consent, except where required by UAE law.

8. Liability

Orbit Training Centre is not liable for any indirect, incidental, or consequential losses arising from training activities. The maximum liability shall not exceed the fees paid for the relevant course.
