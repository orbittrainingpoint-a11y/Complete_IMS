By registering, the company representative confirms they are authorized to enter into this agreement on behalf of the company. All training fees are quoted in UAE Dirhams (AED) and are subject to 5% VAT. Corporate invoices are payable within 30 days of the invoice date unless otherwise agreed in writing. Cancellations or substitutions of nominated trainees must be communicated in writing at least 7 working days before the course start date.
